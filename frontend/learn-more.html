About Résumé Intelligence

The future of recruitment, powered by advanced AI and machine learning

Our Mission

To eliminate bias, reduce time-to-hire, and help companies find the perfect candidate every time.

Why We Built This

Traditional recruitment is broken. Recruiters spend hours manually reviewing resumes, qualified candidates get overlooked, and hiring decisions are often based on gut feeling rather than data. We decided to fix this with artificial intelligence.

Our Approach

We combine state-of-the-art natural language processing with machine learning models trained on millions of resumes and job descriptions to provide objective, data-driven insights that augment human decision-making.

Our AI Stack

  • Natural Language Processing: spaCy and custom transformer models for understanding context
  • Machine Learning: Random Forest and Gradient Boosting models for prediction
  • Embedding Models: SBERT for semantic similarity between texts
  • Continuous Learning: Models retrain weekly with new data
  • Bias Mitigation: Active monitoring and adjustment to reduce demographic bias
