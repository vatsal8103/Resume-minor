Guided Platform Tour

See how Résumé Intelligence works in just 3 simple steps

Step 1: Define Your Role

Start by telling us about the position you're hiring for.

01

Job Description Input

Paste your job description or let our AI help you craft one based on role titles and requirements.

Our AI analyzes:

  • Required skills and technologies
  • Experience level and years
  • Education and certifications
  • Soft skills and cultural fit indicators
  • Location and remote work preferences

Step 2: Add Candidates

Upload resumes or add candidates manually for analysis.

02

Multiple Input Methods

Choose how you want to add candidate information:

  • Batch Upload: Drag and drop multiple resumes (PDF, DOCX, TXT)
  • Manual Entry: Paste resume text or fill in a form
  • LinkedIn Import: Connect to import profiles directly from LinkedIn
  • ATS Integration: Sync with your existing applicant tracking system

Each resume is processed in seconds to extract skills, experience, and qualifications.

Step 3: Get Results & Insights

Receive ranked candidates with detailed analytics and recommendations.

03

Intelligent Matching Results

Our AI provides more than just scores - you get actionable insights:

  • Ranked Shortlist: Candidates sorted by overall match score
  • Skill Gap Analysis: See exactly what skills are missing
  • Role Fit Predictions: AI confidence in candidate suitability
  • Recommendation Engine: Suggestions for next steps
  • Detailed Breakdown: Understand why each candidate scored as they did

Use filters to narrow down candidates by status, skills, location, and more.
